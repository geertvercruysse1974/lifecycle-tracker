Questions

Do I need a credit card?

No. After your free month you receive an invoice, and you choose monthly or yearly billing.

What happens when my trial or subscription ends?

You get a short grace period. After that your access is paused. Your data is kept so you can pick up where you left off when you renew.

Is my data safe?

Each company can only see its own data, access requires a personal login, and the data is stored in the European Union. Read the privacy notice and terms for details.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. You can export your assets as CSV at any time, and ask us to delete your account and data.

What if I store more assets than my plan allows?

You move to the next plan. The price follows the number of assets you store.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Contact us and we will take care of it.
